## 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### A. Account Information
* **Registration Data:** Username, display name, optional email address, and a securely hashed password (argon2id).
* **Security Credentials:** Passwords are hashed using argon2id with per-user salts. Plaintext passwords are never stored.
* **Session and Access Tokens:** Token identifiers maintain secure, authenticated sessions.

### B. Learning and Testing Data
* **Lab Progress:** Completion status of authentication security exercises and modules.
* **Assessment Results:** Scores and results from security testing simulations.
* **Threat Modeling Data:** Attack surface profiles and threat actor profiles you create within the platform.

### C. Usage Analytics and Telemetry
* **Interaction Logs:** Features accessed, time spent on modules, error logs, and loading durations.

### A. Special Administrative Access
**You acknowledge and explicitly agree that authorized system administrators may access, view, or process stored user data solely for the purposes of support maintenance, legal compliance, abuse prevention, and platform security checks. Access is restricted, logged, and auditable.**

### B. Ownership of Content
You retain full ownership of your lab work and assessment data. You grant AuthShield Lab a limited license to host and process your content solely for delivering the Service.

### C. Data Protection and Security Controls
* **Password Hashing:** argon2id with per-user salts. Plaintext passwords are never stored.
* **Session Management:** Sessions expire after inactivity and can be manually terminated.
* **Access Isolation:** Users can only access their own data. One user can never view another user's information.
* **Audit Logging:** Security-relevant events are logged for abuse prevention and compliance.
* **Account Lockout:** Repeated failed login attempts trigger temporary account lockout.
